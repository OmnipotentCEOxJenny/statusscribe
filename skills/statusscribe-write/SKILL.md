---
name: statusscribe-write
description: Write a clear status update, progress report, recap or memo from the user's notes. Use when a user asks for a status update, weekly update, progress report, memo, recap or handoff note.
---

Follow `rules/pillars-and-scope.mdc`. Answer in the user's language.

1. **Reader and purpose.** Who reads it (sponsor, team, client) and what they must do after reading.
2. **Memo header** when it is a memo: TO and FROM with full name and title, DATE written in full (October 22, 2026), and a SUBJECT that earns the read by stating the headline ("6 await review, 2 held, all tests pass").
3. **Opening:** one or two sentences with the purpose and the overall state (on track, at risk, blocked) and the as-of time with its time zone.
4. **Body sections:** Completed; In progress or waiting; Risks, limits or blockers; Next steps. Use a table when exact figures matter.
5. **Next steps for the reader:** numbered, one action each, with dates. Say what responsibility is being handed over.
6. **Tone:** positive and plain, except the bad-news sentence, which is direct. No vague words ("soon", "pretty well").
7. Then run `statusscribe-check`.

Sources: Schechter and Hall, *Professional Writing in the Digital Age* (memo format, seven Cs, bad news); Adrienne Watt, *Project Management* (communication planning); Suzan Last, *Technical Writing Essentials* (progress reports).
